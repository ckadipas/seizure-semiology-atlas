## [Unreleased]

## 1.5.0 — 2026-09-08

- Simplified Signs, Weighted evidence, and Sources, with one search and consistent filters.
- Aligned regional ordering and foldable groups across Signs and Weighted evidence; retained separate localization and lateralization views and ranking by score, papers, or reported values.
- Ordered classification menus, evidence classes, and anatomy choices, with searchable option lists.
- Added recorded author citations and DOI links to compact paper sections with full-width, scrollable results.
- Consolidated repeated statistical context while preserving differences in populations, subgroups, comparisons, units, timepoints, endpoints, and uncertainty.
- Displayed the selected Brodmann number and name beneath the map, and added a short guide to navigation and scores.
- Improved discovery of source-supported region and classification relationships through the shared evidence graph.

This release preserves source-specific results and the existing weighting method. Missing or unresolved source relationships remain visible without inferred assignments.
