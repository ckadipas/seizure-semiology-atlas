# Paper intake

Open the [**Submit a paper for intake** form](https://github.com/ckadipas/seizure-semiology-atlas/issues/new?template=new-paper.yml) and provide one paper using any of these options:

- Upload the paper PDF.
- Upload page images or photographs.
- Paste a DOI or stable publisher or repository link.

Drag files into the paper field or use its attachment button, then submit the issue. A PDF or page images are sufficient; a DOI is not required alongside an upload. The note is optional. Submitters do not need to extract findings, classify the study, assess reliability, or supply a formatted citation.

The issue and its attachments are public. Upload files you have permission to share publicly, and keep private information out of the submission. Working source copies and review artifacts are retained in the private evidence archive.

The issue registers a nomination only. After a maintainer applies the approved label, the public workflow records it for private Gate A preparation. It does not download the source, review scientific content, edit the atlas, or publish scientific results.

The owner approves the exact source and review scope at Gate A. After private source review, the owner reviews the proposed records and integration diff at Gate B. Deterministic integration and deployment require their own authorized steps. A submission cannot change the public atlas by itself.
