# Paper intake

Submitters only need to open a **Submit a paper for intake** issue and provide
one of the following:

- a DOI;
- a stable publisher or repository link; or
- an attachment they have permission to share in a public GitHub issue.

The note is optional. Submitters do not need to extract findings, classify the
study, assess reliability, or supply a formatted citation. Prefer a DOI or
publisher link for copyrighted articles.

The issue registers a nomination only. After a maintainer applies the approved
label, the public workflow records it for private Gate A preparation. It does
not download the source, review scientific content, edit the atlas, or publish
anything.

Source files and review artifacts remain in the private evidence repository.
The owner approves the exact source and review scope at Gate A. After private
source review, the owner reviews the proposed records and integration diff at
Gate B. Deterministic integration and deployment require their own authorized
steps. A submission cannot change the public atlas by itself.
